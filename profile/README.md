# Setun Labs

An independent lab building ternary, on-device AI. Specialists, not generalists. Measured, never estimated.

**[setunlabs.ai](https://setunlabs.ai)**

## The work

- **Models**: the Grimm Series. Classes of open, ternary-weight, mixture-of-experts specialists. A math specialist proof of concept is in development now.
- **Apps**: Pocket AI Hub. One app to run those models on your phone and recruit your computer for the big jobs. In final on-device testing.
- **Tools**: the open-source plumbing underneath both.

Nothing here is released yet, and everything says so. When each piece is real and measured, it publishes.

## The creed

Three stars. One line. Mechanism before scale. Refutation over abandonment. One domain, zero doubt.
